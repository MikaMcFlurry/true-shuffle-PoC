# Design System: true-shuffle

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Radio on the Sideboard"**

The screen is the front of a 1950s German tube radio. One walnut front under lacquer; a golden speaker cloth across the top carrying the maker's emblem, "true-shuffle" in cast brass letters between two brass trim bars; a cream glass dial lit from behind with every station printed on it and the green magic eye seated at its end; the program card behind a brass-framed window; two bakelite knobs; one slot of ivory piano keys flanked by two shorter bakelite keys. Everything the listener touches on the radio is a physical part of it. Pages that do not fit on the front are printed program sheets (Programmheft paper) or the radio's own back panel (hardboard with vent holes, screws and a riveted brass type plate); paper carries print, not keys.

The silhouette answers to the screen. On a phone the screen is the face in close-up: walnut to the screen's edges, no cabinet outline of its own, and the face ends in the plinth's moulding. From 980px the whole table radio stands on a walnut sideboard: landscape, softly rounded lit shoulders, straight sides, a darker plinth with two feet. Density is that of a real appliance: a glance of two or three seconds reads the lit station on the dial and the song on the card; a tap on a printed station name sends the red pointer gliding there. Every station stays printed on the glass, the playing one struck forward and lit, the others printed and quiet. The world has two illuminations, same token names, other values: **Day** (a pale plaster wall, the radio lit by the room) and **Night** (a dark room where wood, cloth and paper fall into shadow and only the dial lamp, the magic eye and the paper glow carry light). Night follows `prefers-color-scheme: dark` unless the user pins `data-illumination`.

Confirmed rejections (owner, 2026-09-27): app cards, streaming-app chrome, a numbers cockpit, anything playful or cold; the previous car head-unit world (black VFD glass, machined keys, dot-matrix type); a script badge on a screwed plate; a radio silhouette that merely follows the viewport (a tall wooden phone case, a flat pinboard panel).

**Key Characteristics:**
- One object: the front is a single walnut body; dial, eye, window, knobs and keys are seated in it, never floating tiles.
- A maker's emblem, not a sign: cast brass lower-case Jost letters on the cloth, the trim bars level with the hyphen.
- Real material: walnut and cloth are sourced CC0 rasters; paper grain, brushed brass, plaster and hardboard are small inline SVG noise filters.
- Print, not UI: dial lettering is condensed caps printed on glass; paper pages are set like a printed running order, and their actions are printed too.
- One signal red for "this is the one": the pointer, the lit station, pilot lamps, the latched key's legend, the chosen mark on a printed scale.
- One living light: the green magic eye.

## Colors

A warm, lamp-lit palette of walnut, brass, cream glass, ivory, bakelite and paper, with a single signal red and a single phosphor green.

### Primary
- **Dial Signal Red** (`lit`): the pointer needle, the lit and held station name, its number box and frequency, the station's scale mark, the round mark on the card's scale. Reserved for "the one that plays".
- **Deep Signal Red** (`lit-deep`, `lit-ink`): red set as ink: the latched piano key's legend, lit wave-band lamps' text, the chosen tick on the printed mix scale, danger actions and error notes on paper. On the back panel it becomes `board-lit` (a pale coral) to hold contrast on hardboard.

### Secondary
- **Lacquered Brass** (`brass-hi`, `brass`, `brass-lo`): the trim line inlaid round the desktop front, the rings round the cloth, dial bezel, eye escutcheon and window, knob rings, the magic-eye bezel, the brass borders of the status strip and the rating slip. Always a gradient of the three stops or a ring over a dark ground, never a flat panel.
- **Cast Brass** (`emblem-face-hi`, `emblem-face-lo`): the emblem only. Its face is one even casting, `color-mix(in oklab, emblem-face-hi 45%, emblem-face-lo)`, shared by the letters and both trim bars; the chamfer and the shadow carry the metal, not a gradient.
- **Key Brass** (`key-brass`, `key-brass-ink`): the back panel's riveted type plate and the toggle lever's nut.

### Tertiary
- **Magic Eye Green** (`eye`, `eye-lo`, `eye-hi`): only the EM-tube fan. It is the radio's one living light; it opens when playing, closes while tuning, dims when off.

### Neutral
- **Plaster Wall** (`wall`, `wall-2`, `wall-ink`): the room behind the radio; the page background under a plaster-noise texture.
- **Walnut** (`walnut`, `walnut-deep`): the front and the sideboard under the walnut raster; `walnut-deep` is the plinth. `wood-ink` and `wood-ink-2` are the pale legends printed on wood (knob names, detents).
- **Speaker Cloth Gold** (`cloth`): the base under the cloth raster, a veil and a sparse gold thread.
- **Backlit Dial Glass** (`glass-hi` → `glass` → `glass-lo` → `glass-edge`): a radial lamp falloff, brightest at the centre; `halo` is the soft glow behind printed dial letters and the clear patch under each count. `print` and `print-2` are the brown-black dial inks.
- **Ivory** (`ivory-hi`, `ivory`, `ivory-lo`, `ivory-front`, `ivory-lip`): the four play keys' faces, front lips and pressed faces; `engrave` is the cut legend.
- **Bakelite** (`bakelite-hi`, `bakelite`, `bakelite-lo`, `bakelite-ink`): knob caps, the desktop feet, and the MENÜ and VERLAUF keys with pale filled legends. **Key Slot** (`slot`): the dark slot all keys sit in.
- **Programmheft Paper** (`paper`, `paper-2`, `card`, `field`): program sheets, slips, form fields. Inks `ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`; hairlines `rule`, `rule-strong`.
- **Window Paper** (`window-paper`, `window-ink`, `window-ink-2`, `window-lit`): the program card seen through the cabinet's window. By day it equals `card`; by night it falls into the room's dark (about L* 60) and its inks are raised to hold AA contrast on it.
- **Hardboard** (`board`, `board-2`, `board-ink`, `board-field`): the back panel (menu). A page switches to it by re-pointing the paper ink and rule tokens, so every sheet component works on it unchanged.
- **Focus** (`focus-on-light` blue, `focus-on-dark` pale gold): the only non-period colours, used solely for the 3px focus outline. Each surface sets `--focus` for its own ground (dial, ivory keys and paper: blue; cabinet, bakelite keys and back panel: gold).

### Named Rules
**The One Signal Rule.** Red means "the station that plays", "this key is down" or "this is the chosen position"; it never decorates. Pilot lamps (a red radial bulb), the pointer, the lit station, the latched legend and a chosen scale tick are its homes, plus error and danger text.

**The Two Illuminations Rule.** Every colour exists for Day and Night under the same name. New surfaces use tokens, never literal hex, so that Night comes for free. Night darkens the wood (`wood-dim` 0.74) and cloth (`cloth-dim` 0.6), dims the window paper, and raises the dial glow (`spill`), the eye glow and a warm `paper-glow`.

**The Card-in-the-Dark Rule.** Paper seen inside the cabinet at night is lit only by the room: it drops to about L* 60 and carries its own inks (`window-ink`, `window-ink-2`, `window-lit`), each at least 4.5:1 on it. Paper outside the cabinet keeps the sheet's inks.

## Typography

**Body and Emblem Font:** Jost Variable (with Jost, Futura, Century Gothic, system-ui)
**Dial / Label Font:** Barlow Condensed 500 and 600 (with Jost)

Both are self-hosted through @fontsource (`@fontsource-variable/jost` wght, `@fontsource/barlow-condensed` latin 500/600). There is no third face.

**Character:** A period geometric sans for the maker's emblem and everything printed on paper, a narrow condensed face for everything printed on glass, ivory, bakelite or brass. It reads like a 1950s radio and its program booklet.

- **Display** (Jost 600, clamp(28px, 8.4vw, 36px), 1.06, -0.01em): program-sheet masthead titles. On the back panel's plate: 26px, 0.02em.
- **Headline** (Jost 650, 18px, 1.3): run-in section heads, set inline with their text followed by ". ".
- **Title** (Jost 600, 17.5px, 1.2): song title on the program card, two lines max.
- **Action, primary** (Jost 700, 17.5px, -0.005em): the main action printed on paper. **Action** (Jost 500, 15.5px, underlined): every other printed action; back and small 14.5px.
- **Body** (Jost 400, 16px, 1.45); **Lede** (15.5px, 1.6, max 62ch); **Hint** (13.5px, 1.5).
- **Label** (Barlow Condensed 600, 14.5px, 0.08em, uppercase): printed legends on paper; the mix scale's positions (500, 0.06em). Piano and bakelite key legends 11.5px, 0.04em (13px, 0.08em from 980px; 10.5px under 380px); terminal legends 16px.
- **Label small** (Barlow Condensed 600, 12px, 0.18em, uppercase): knob names (KLANG, SENDERWAHL), band legends, wave-band lamps, detents.

### Named Rules
**The Name Rule.** The name is always "true-shuffle", lower case with a hyphen, never "True Shuffle" and never set in capitals. It must never sit inside an uppercase-transformed element (dial lettering, key legends, terminal legends, tags). On the cloth it is the cast Jost emblem; nowhere is it set in a script.

**The Printed-Where-It-Lives Rule.** Condensed uppercase belongs to glass, ivory, bakelite, brass and printed legends; running text and actions on paper are Jost in sentence case. Don't set paragraphs or paper actions in Barlow Condensed, or legends in Jost.

## Layout

**Phone (to 979px): the face in close-up.** A screen that holds the radio drops the shell's padding: the walnut runs to the screen's edges, safe-area aware, with no outline, radius or wall beside it. The face is a column: cloth, dial, window (program card), extra, keyboard with the knobs at its lower corners, then the plinth (24px plus the safe-area inset). Body padding 14px 12px 18px, gap 12px; every part except the cloth is centred at a maximum of 680px, the cloth runs full width. A face that fills the screen (`cabinet--fill`, 100svh) gives its spare height to the cloth, up to 46svh. The phone dial is a vertical scale down the right edge with the string and pointer on it; the magic eye looks out through the glass at the head of that scale, and the first station begins below it; station rows are at least 44px tall. Pages after the face keep their own margins (10px, max 528px, centred from 560px). Other pages use the `shell`: max 560px, 10px side padding (16px from 420px), gap 20px.

**Desktop (from 980px): the table radio on a sideboard.** The front is landscape (aspect 1.62 when it fills), padding 26px 20px 22px, gap 16px, a container for its own queries. Cloth across the full width with the emblem centred; one dial strip under it with the eye's escutcheon (72px) at its end cap, the row reaching 8px into the margins; along the bottom the Klang knob, the keyboard (max 600px, 540px when flanked) and the Senderwahl knob, in one row from a 710px front, knobs under the keyboard's corners below that. A narrow front (up to 700px) lets the cloth shrink to 88px; a dense dial takes it to 76px. Under the plinth two feet stand on the sideboard, a 38px walnut piece running from the browser's left edge to the sheet column (or across the whole window when the sheet lies below).
- **980–1199px:** the radio at most 940px wide, centred; the program card and the sheet stacked below it at max 640px, gap 36px.
- **From 1200px:** a grid of `minmax(0, 940px)` and a side column of 320px (400px from 1400px); the radio is sticky at 24px while the side column scrolls. The program card leaves the cabinet and heads the side column above the sheet.
- Sign-in, switching on and faults show the radio alone at max 860px.

**The desktop dial.** Stations in bands under printed band legends (40px), as many columns as the glass holds (min column 100px, up to 8, bands evened out so twelve read as two of six). Past two rows it prints dense: min column 84px, up to 8 columns on a glass of 760px or more (6 below), rows about 34px, 44px on a touch screen (`pointer: coarse`).

**Program sheets:** padding 18px 18px 30px, sections 26px apart, section inner gap 10px, stacks 12px. Rows are 56px minimum; terminals 58px; action lines 52–58px; every tap target is at least 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical and lit from above: highlights on top edges (inset white or cream 1px lines), shade under lips and in slots (inset dark shadows), and cast shadows only where a thing stands on something. On the phone the face is the screen and casts nothing; on the desktop the radio stands on the sideboard, the feet cast onto its top face and the sideboard casts onto the wall. Nothing floats on a neutral drop shadow; every shadow belongs to a material (lacquer, cast brass, brass edge, key lip, slot, paper lying on a surface). Night deepens `cast` and dims wood via `wood-dim`.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lacquered front** (`inset 0 2px 1px rgba(255,238,208,.6), inset 0 14px 18px -12px rgba(255,230,190,.34), inset 0 -8px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.42), 0 2px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`): the desktop front; a 1px brass trim line at 60% opacity is inlaid 7px inside it.
- **Sideboard** (`0 1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.35), 0 20px 30px -18px var(--cast)`): the furniture the radio stands on; the feet cast `0 3px 3px -1px rgba(0,0,0,.6)` onto it.
- **Cast emblem** (`drop-shadow(0 -1px 0 var(--emblem-edge)) drop-shadow(0 1px 0 var(--emblem-side)) drop-shadow(1px 2.5px 1px var(--emblem-cast))`): a lit top chamfer, a dark side and a crisp shadow on the weave, on letters and trim bars alike.
- **Brass ring** (`0 0 0 2px var(--brass), 0 0 0 3px rgba(30,16,4,.55–.6)`): cloth, dial bezel and the eye's escutcheon.
- **Slot** (`inset 0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.9)` over a `#050201 → slot → #24160c` gradient): the keyboard, with a 10px shade from its upper lip.
- **Key joint** (inset 1px lit left edge, shaded right edge); **key pressed** (`inset 0 5px 7px rgba(30,18,4,.55)` plus a 7px sink; bakelite `rgba(0,0,0,.7)`).
- **Window paper** (`inset 0 4px 9px rgba(40,20,5,.42)`): the card seen through the brass window.
- **Sheet on a surface** (`0 1px 1px rgba(30,20,5,.2), 0 14px 30px -16px rgba(30,20,5,.55)`); the desktop side sheet adds a second page edge.
- **Glow** (`0 0 38px var(--spill)` dial, `0 0 22px var(--eye-glow)` eye, `0 0 7px rgba(230,70,40,.6)` pilot lamp): light, not elevation; zero or faint by day, visible by night.

### Named Rules
**The Lit-From-Above Rule.** Every raised part has a light top edge and a shaded foot; every recessed part has its shadow at the top. If a shadow does not name a material and a light source, it does not belong.

**The Stands-On-Something Rule.** A cast shadow needs a surface to fall on: the emblem on the cloth, the feet on the sideboard, the sideboard on the wall, a sheet on the desk. The phone face, being the screen, casts nothing.

## Shapes

Soft period forms. The desktop front has broad rounded shoulders and a nearly square foot (76px 76px 14px 14px / 60px 60px 12px 12px); the cloth follows it (56px 56px 6px 6px / 42px 42px 6px 6px), a plain 10px on the phone. The dial bezel is 14px with 9px glass; the eye's escutcheon is a 12px square; the brass window 8px with 3px paper. The plinth is a straight moulding (rounded 6px/5px at its foot on the desktop), the feet 58 × 15px with rounded toes. Paper is nearly square (2px), keys are 2px with square feet where they meet the slot (2px 2px 0 0). The emblem's trim bars are as thick as the hyphen's stroke (0.11em, 1px radius), set on its line. Everything round is truly round: knobs, eye, screws, pilot lamps, detent dots (50%). Screws are small radial-gradient brass heads with a rotated slot, each at a different angle; they hold the window and the back panel, never the emblem.

## Components

### Maker's emblem (signature)
"true-shuffle" in cast brass on the cloth: Jost 560 lower case, one even face (`color-mix(in oklab, emblem-face-hi 45%, emblem-face-lo)`), the cast-emblem chamfer and shadow. Two brass trim bars of the same casting cross the cloth either side, flexing to fill it, level with the hyphen so the hyphen reads as a piece of the bar, with a 0.4em gap at the letters (0.42em on desktop). No plate, no screws, no gradient text. Its focus ring carries a 4px dark band inside it so it reads on the weave.

### Piano keys and bakelite keys (the cabinet keyboard)
One continuous dark slot: MENÜ and VERLAUF either side (`minmax(46px, .6fr)` each), the four play keys between (`4fr`).
- **Ivory play keys:** 2px 2px 0 0, min height 60px, legend cut just above an 8px front lip; ivory gradient face, `ivory-front`/`ivory-lip` lip, `engrave` legend with a shaded top edge and a lit lower edge.
- **Bakelite keys** (MENÜ, VERLAUF): shorter (50px) dark bakelite, aligned to the slot's foot, pale `bakelite-ink` legends cut the same way; gold focus.
- **Hover / Focus:** brightness 1.04; focus outline inset -6px.
- **Pressed / Latched:** sinks 7px, lip disappears below the slot edge, the slot's shadow falls on its face; a latched key (pause) stays down and its legend turns red (`lit-key`). The pause key is a latching toggle named by its engraving.
- **Power key:** larger (64px) with a red pilot lamp beside a 16px legend.

### Printed actions (paper)
Paper carries no keys; keys belong to the cabinet. On program sheets, the side card, the back panel and the rating slip every action is printed in the sheet's own type:
- **Primary:** Jost 700 17.5px in `ink`, no underline (a 2px underline on hover). Full width on a line of its own, ruled above and below in `rule-strong`, 52px.
- **Secondary:** Jost 500 15.5px in `ink-2`, underlined in `rule-strong` 4px below; hover inks it and the underline.
- **Danger:** `lit-ink`, underline in the same red.
- **Chosen** (rating slip): bold `lit-ink` after a 16px printed tick.
- **Station line** (`acts`): the primary action on the left, a secondary reference on the right, on one 58px line ruled above and below. **Lone line** (`act`): Jost 600 16px across the sheet between rules, `row-hover` on hover.
- Every target at least 44 × 44px; no leaders, no fills, no pilot lamp beside an action (lamps on paper belong only to detent selectors, marking a set position).

### The dial (signature)
A brass-ringed dark bezel holding the backlit glass with a bright top edge and a diagonal reflection band; one strip. Stations are rows of number box (phone only), condensed uppercase name and a small frequency-style count. States: waiting (`print-2`), selected with the knob (600), lit (red, scale 1.05, red glow), held in pause (red, not glowing). Hover underlines the name. Along the foot of the glass a row of wave-band lamps (7px dots, lit red when on).
- **Phone:** a vertical scale down the right with the string; each station printed over its own short line of scale.
- **Desktop:** bands under printed legends, each station centred over a 2px scale mark (red for the lit one), its count on a clear patch of `halo` glass so the pointer never runs through it. **Dense** past two rows: 13px names, the count on its own line under the name, up to 8 columns.
- **Pointer:** a red needle with a dark rider on a thin string, gliding to the lit station over 1150ms with `--ease-string` (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.26, 0.5, 1)`), a slight string overshoot. On the desktop it is a fine line behind the print, strong only where it meets the lit station's scale. While switching on (Suchlauf) it sweeps the scale, 3.4s ease-in-out alternate.

### Magic eye
A 52px brass-ringed lens with two green half-disc fans. States: open (playing), weak, tuned, tuning (closes and reopens during a pointer glide), off. Transitions 900ms `--ease`. Seated in the dial: on the phone at 0.7 scale in a round window through the glass at the head of the scale, ringed dark then brass; on the desktop full size in its own square escutcheon at the dial's end cap, dark bezel ringed in the same brass line.

### Knobs and detents
Bakelite caps (66px, 60px under 380px) in a brass conic ring with an inlaid ivory pointer line and printed ticks round them; names in label small, `wood-ink` on wood, `ink` on paper. Klang has three detents printed round it (Entdecker, Ausgewogen, Vertraut), each a 44px target with a dot that lights red when chosen. Knobs turn by drag.

### Mix scale (paper)
Where no knob belongs, the station sheet prints Klang as the knob's three detents: a radiogroup of three equal positions on a strip of printed scale under a `rule-strong` line, each 56px tall, labels in condensed uppercase `ink-2`. The chosen one is bold `ink` with a 3 × 14px red tick; the others carry a 1 × 8px hairline. A position is marked only when the mix stands exactly on that detent; a mix turned between detents on the radio marks none, and the Mischung readout above prints the true mix. Choosing a position snaps the mix to that detent's value.

### Program card
Behind the brass window (8px padding, four screws) on the phone: paper texture on `window-paper`, cover art 64px (2px radius, fades in 420ms), song in title style, artist, reason, and the round as a printed tick scale with a red 3px mark. On the desktop it leaves the cabinet and heads the side column: the window's brass and screws drop away, it is printed on the sheet's own paper and inks, closed by a 3px double rule onto the sheet below (a free card of its own above the back panel).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build every control on the radio as a physical part of it: an ivory or bakelite key in the slot, a bakelite knob in a brass ring, a lever, a printed station on the glass, a screw terminal on the back panel.
- **Do** print actions on paper in the sheet's type: the primary one bold, the others underlined, danger in red ink, each at least 44px.
- **Do** keep every station printed on the dial at all times; mark the playing one by striking it forward and lighting it red, not by hiding the others.
- **Do** let the silhouette answer the screen: the face edge to edge ending in its plinth on a phone; the landscape table radio with shoulders, plinth, feet and sideboard from 980px.
- **Do** use tokens for every colour so Day and Night both work; set `--focus` for the surface's ground.
- **Do** set lists and settings as a printed Programmheft: rules, run-in heads, condensed legends.
- **Do** keep tap targets at least 44px.
- **Do** keep `--ease-string` (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.26, 0.5, 1)`) on the dial pointer's glide: it is the one sanctioned exception to the no-bounce rule (recorded in `.impeccable/config.json` `detector.ignoreValues`), and reduced motion reduces it to 1ms.
- **Do** give every shipping raster a provenance `.json` beside it (origin, licence, processing).

### Don't:
- **Don't** write the name as "True Shuffle" or set it in capitals, or place it inside an uppercase-transformed element.
- **Don't** mount the emblem on a plate, fix it with screws or set it in a script face; it is cast Jost letters on the cloth.
- **Don't** put keys, key slots or dotted leaders on paper, or mark a paper action with a pilot lamp.
- **Don't** use overshoot or bounce easing anywhere except the dial pointer; everything else moves on `--ease` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) at 120ms or 220ms, the eye at 900ms.
- **Don't** bring back the car head-unit world: black VFD glass, machined silver keys, dot-matrix display type.
- **Don't** use app cards, floating tiles, streaming-app chrome or a numbers cockpit; progress is a small printed scale with a red mark.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue; red is the signal, green is only the magic eye, brass is metal.
- **Don't** use flat brass panels; brass is a lit gradient or ring, and the emblem's one cast face is carried by its chamfer and shadow.
- **Don't** add emoji, Unicode glyphs as icons, or generic drop shadows that name no material.
