# Product Decision Contract

## Evaluation dimensions

Apply only relevant dimensions:

- user value and criticality/frequency;
- core-loop/journey fit, coherence and simplicity;
- differentiation and reason to exist;
- trust, control, privacy and recovery clarity;
- learnability, accessibility and input/device fit;
- performance/feel and reliability expectations;
- feasibility on intended platforms;
- implementation, infrastructure, moderation, content and support burden;
- security, abuse, compliance, safety, migration and reputation risk;
- strength/freshness of evidence.

For games also evaluate controls/game feel, simulation/physics, frame time/input latency, progression/economy, replayability/content burden, networking/authority, save/reconnect, onboarding/difficulty and control customization.

## Required handoff

Return:

1. `UNDERLYING_GOAL` and intended users;
2. preserved `NON_NEGOTIABLES` and anti-goals;
3. one direction verdict;
4. material `KEEP/IMPROVE/REDESIGN/REMOVE/ADD/PIVOT` decisions with concise evidence;
5. chosen core scope and explicit exclusions;
6. critical journeys and degraded/recovery states;
7. observable quality/success criteria;
8. benchmark panel when used;
9. assumptions, risks and evidence gaps;
10. accepted, rejected, deferred, or Studio-owned packet dispositions when Repository Intelligence was used;
11. no product question unless an irreversible preference truly cannot be inferred.

The handoff should let Engineering proceed without another product meeting.
