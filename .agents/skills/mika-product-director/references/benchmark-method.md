# Benchmark Method

Use a small panel, normally three to eight references, only when comparison adds decision value. Select by quality dimension, not prestige or category coverage.

Useful dimensions include onboarding/time-to-value, information architecture, platform craft, accessibility, trust/status, collaboration, developer experience, game feel, vehicle/physics behavior, networking, open-world streaming, performance, recovery and live operations.

For each selected reference record:

- reference and exact dimension;
- why it is relevant;
- current primary evidence or verified contribution scope;
- retrieval time and the condition that requires revalidation;
- lesson and project-specific observable criterion;
- what must not be copied.

Prefer current observable product behavior, official documentation/case studies and official engineering/design material. A logo wall, award, marketing superlative or remembered reputation is not capability evidence. Distinguish a defined case study/contribution from an unspecified client relationship.

Never clone proprietary code, content, assets, branding or distinctive trade dress. Drop weak, stale or irrelevant references rather than expanding the panel. Domain examples are discovery cues only: for a racing game, investigate current leaders in driving feel, deformation, netcode and world streaming; for a SaaS product, investigate the exact workflow/trust/accessibility dimension at issue. Do not freeze candidate brands into permanent truth.
