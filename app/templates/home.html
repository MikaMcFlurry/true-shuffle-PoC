Two ways to run a deck

Live Mode

true-shuffle decides what plays next and moves the deck forward on its own — when a track ends, and when you skip. Audio still comes from the streaming service, not from us.

Needs a service that allows playback control.

Copy Mode

Writes the shuffled order into a real playlist on the service. You press play there and listen straight through. Works even where playback control is not available.

Needs a service that allows creating playlists.
