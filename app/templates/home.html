Two ways to run a deck

Handoff Mode

Nothing stays open

The shuffled order is written into a real playlist on the service. You press play there and listen however you normally do — phone, car, speaker. Where the service exposes a listening history, true-shuffle reads back how far you got, so the deck keeps your place and finishes exactly once.

Live Mode

Depends on the service

true-shuffle decides what plays next and moves the deck forward the moment a track ends or you skip. On Spotify this runs from the server, so nothing of ours needs to be open. On Apple Music and YouTube it runs in the browser tab, because neither service lets anything else control playback.
